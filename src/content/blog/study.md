# Welcome #

## Introduction ##

Welcome to my humble blog. One of the reason why I make this blog is to provide me with an online platform to record and potentially share my **Study Notes** to the outside world. Of course this is not the only reason why I made this blog but it is one of the many motivation for sure.

### What I study ###

In the course of my life, naturally will I learn about so many new things. But to record all these learnings will make this category of post to be ill defined. For that reason, what I consider as **Study Notes** worthy are those knowledge, ideas, questions and confusions that I obtain through the course of reading scientific publications such as journal articles, textbooks, conference papers etc.
At the moment, I am interested in the following topics:
- Entanglement based Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)
- Quantum memory and quantum repeater
- Optical Quantum Communication
